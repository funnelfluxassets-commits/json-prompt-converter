Why JSON?

JSON prompts provide a mathematical structure to creativity. Unlike natural language, which can be ambiguous, JSON forces the AI to acknowledge every specific parameter independently.

⚡

Superior Precision

By separating the Subject from the Environment and Lighting, you eliminate "concept bleeding" where colors or styles from one part of your prompt infect others.

🛠️

Easy Tweaking

Want to change just the lighting? Simply edit the lighting value. Want to swap the camera angle? Change the camera key. No need to rephrase your entire description.

Making Precise Changes

Once you've generated your prompt, you can manually fine-tune the JSON before pasting it into your AI generator. Here's how to maximize your results:

  • Subject Attributes: Modify the description or appearance to change physical traits without affecting the background.
  • Environment Scale: Adjust the details array to add or remove specific objects from the scene.
  • Meta Tuning: Use the meta block to lock in aspect ratios or specific rendering resolutions across different versions.
