Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Data Collection

JSONGen is a privacy-first tool. All prompt conversion logic happens locally in your browser. We do not store, collect, or transmit your prompts to any server.

2. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We may use local storage to remember your preferences (like the selected Nano Banana version), but this data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

This site is a static application. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking scripts that compromise your privacy.

4. Security

Because your data never leaves your browser, it is as secure as your own device. No tokens, API keys, or personal data are required to use JSONGen.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at support@funnelfluxassets.com.
